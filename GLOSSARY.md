# Glossary

- **Transfer budget**: money available for buying players. Starts at about €1.2M for Oldham (community figure, unverified in game). Sales feed it; the exact mechanics are unverified.
- **Wage budget**: weekly ceiling on the wage bill. Oldham's value is unknown. Keep the bill at or below 90% of it.
- **OVR / POT**: a player's current overall rating and potential rating. POT drives resale value and development.
- **Dynamic OVR**: FC 27 system where a player's rating changes with form and development.
- **Growth Profile**: FC 27 profile deciding how a youngster's rating develops.
- **High-POT player**: a young player whose POT is well above their OVR; the core of the trading model.
- **Loan player**: a player on loan at Oldham, owned by another club. Never build the plan on keeping one.
- **Free agent**: a player with no club, signed without a transfer fee. Wages still count.
- **Resale value**: what a player is likely to fetch when sold. Decides who earns a new contract.
- **First window**: the first transfer window of the career. Spend at most about 70% of the transfer budget.
- **Director of Football**: staff role that can negotiate transfers for the manager.
- **Mid-block**: a compact defensive shape that sits deep-ish and counters on turnover.
