## 3. Manager Career rules (FC 27)

Primary: [official] https://www.ea.com/games/ea-sports-fc/fc-27/news/pitch-notes-fc27-career-mode-deep-dive (31 Jul 2026), [official] https://www.ea.com/games/ea-sports-fc/fc-27/news/fc-27-career-mode-developer-launch-update (29 Sep 2026), [official] https://www.ea.com/games/ea-sports-fc/fc-27/news/update-v1-1-pitch-notes (TU 1.1, 27 Oct 2026).

- Transfer system: multi-stage deals starting with an enquiry; negotiation only after permission; choose immediate move, next window, or start of next season; agreements can be reopened by rival interest; 2D negotiation screen replaces 3D; player exchanges return; Director of Football can negotiate for you. [official, deep dive]
- Valuations: new model built with TransferRoom; values change across seasons. [official, deep dive via search summary]
- Loans: loan enquiries from player profile; Loan List; loan-back agreements. [official, deep dive]
- Finance: fee installments across seasons, achievement bonuses, buyback/first-refusal/sell-on clauses, automatic wage rises/cuts triggered by results (e.g. promotion), reveal screen shows budget remaining. [official, deep dive]
- Selling: sellers get one last chance to revisit terms; how sales feed the transfer budget (percentage etc.): unverified. A forum user reports money for a future-window deal is deducted from budget immediately [community/unverified] https://forums.ea.com/discussions/fc-27-career-mode-en/manager-career-mode-multiple-major-bugs-eafc27-at-launch/13745751.
- Deadline Day expanded to 20 hours with a 2-hour skip. AI clubs make significantly more moves. [official, deep dive]
- Transfer window dates: unverified (not published in sources found).
- Free agents and contract length rules: unverified. Squad roles are agreed in contract negotiations and set playing-time expectations; unmet ones can have consequences. [official, deep dive] TU 1.1: wage tuning accounts for performance bonuses; veterans no longer demand key roles disproportionately. [official, v1.1 notes]
- Known issues: players requesting transfers despite playtime being met (fix in testing); loan/transfer-listed players get too few offers; newly generated players enter with higher starting OVR than expected (fix in progress). [official, launch update and v1.1 notes]
- Youth/scouting: four scouting stages (Tactical Fit, Future, Financial Assessment, Status); attributes shown instantly (gradual reveal removed); Squad Ranks accuracy depends on staff knowledge; PlayStyles revealed for youth prospects found via Scouting Missions; scout capacity now based on Experience instead of Judgement (TU 1.1). [official]
- Development/training: Training Plan removed except Energy Recovery; Match Fitness indicator (Peak to Unfit); Dynamic OVR from Form, Morale, Match Fitness; Growth Profiles (six types); Dynamic Potential applies to AI teams in Deeper Simulation leagues (expanded from 5 to 10 leagues; whether League Two is among them: unverified). [official, deep dive]
- Other new vs FC 26: Practice Arena in Career, Unexpected Events/Press Conferences toggles (TU 1.1), Liga MX added, up to seven positions per player with six secondary positions penalty-free. [official]
- Unexpected Events: financial mismanagement can bring points deductions (removable or halvable). [official, deep dive]

## 4. Staff

- Coaches reduced to twelve across Goalkeeping, Defence, Midfield, Attack; each department has a Head Coach. Coaches no longer directly improve attributes; they assess Potential (better coaches more accurate) and assess your own players for Squad Ranks. [official, deep dive]
- Scouts assess other clubs' players; scouting network managed in Transfer Hub > Scouting. Scout capacity depends on Experience (TU 1.1). [official]
- Director of Football can negotiate on your behalf. [official]
- Assistant manager, physio, and other roles, hiring process, salaries, and rating effects: unverified (not covered by the sources fetched). Forum user reports staff slots cannot be reassigned between departments [community/unverified] (same forum link as section 3).

## 5. Tactics

- Officially confirmed: Team Strategy screen shows department ratings and player development boosts; Practice Arena inherits your gameplay type and sliders; AI clubs favour players fitting the manager's preferred style. [official, deep dive]
- Official gameplay notes cover Competitive (not Authentic/Career) AI defending changes; EA said Authentic changes would be in the Career deep dive. FUT deep dive adds "Attacking Spatial Awareness" to loosen roles. October notes: win rates by formation are evenly distributed with minimal variance; fullbacks dropping too deep is being addressed. [official, via search summary] https://www.ea.com/games/ea-sports-fc/fc-27/news/pitch-notes-fc27-gameplay-deep-dive , https://www.ea.com/games/ea-sports-fc/fc-27/news/fc27-october-gameplay-developer-notes
- Formation list, 37 Player Roles (3-5 per outfield position), custom tactics: [community] https://fifauteam.com/fc-27-tactics/ , https://www.dexerto.com/wikis/ea-fc-27/best-tactics-and-formations-to-use-in-career-mode/ (Dexerto suggests 4-2-1-3, 5-3-2 Holding, 4-4-2 for Career; community opinion). No official League Two formation advice found: [unverified].
- Fact implication: official sources say no formation dominates; Oldham's listed squad is CM/wide heavy (see section 2).
