# 1 January free-agent capture sheet

Prepared 2026-11-27 for `january-prep.md`. Fill this in on 1 January and send it back for ranking. The pre-filled names are the summer shortlist (Futship, community data from 2026-11-25 and 2026-11-26): ages, ratings and wages are **summer values and will have changed**. The "Futship wage" column is for comparison only. Record the wage **the game asks in your save**, which is the number that counts.

## How to capture

1. Scouting, then Search Players, Transfer Status = **Free Agents**.
2. Search one position at a time, in the order below, sorted by wage (lowest first).
3. For each watch-list name, search by name and mark **Still free?** (Yes, No signed elsewhere, No retired, Not found).
4. Add up to 5 new names per position that are not on the list (blank rows).
5. For the wage, open the negotiation screen and write the ask, not the market value. Note the contract length offered.
6. Pace is on the player card. Growth Profile is on the player's development tab.

## How I will rank (short version, full rules in `january-prep.md` section 4)

1. Wage ask against the room plus the wage freed by the paired sale, within the €15K budget.
2. Gap with one real option first, then promotion order.
3. Upgrade of 2 OVR or more, or cheap cover within 3 OVR.
4. Younger and higher POT breaks ties. Check pace on attackers and wingers.
