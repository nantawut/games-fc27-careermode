## 4. How I will rank them

1. **Wage first.** Reject anyone whose wage is more than the wage freed by the paired sale plus any spare room. No cap stretching.
2. **Upgrade size.** Must beat the current starter in that slot by at least 2 OVR. A cover signing needs to be within 3 OVR of the starter and cost 500 or less.
3. **Gap priority.** Striker, then holding midfielder, then wingers, then goalkeeper, then centre-back (promotion order from `plan.md`). Fix a slot with one real option before upgrading a slot with two.
4. **Age and resale.** Tie-break toward younger, higher-POT players. Over 32 is accepted only for a promotion-critical slot and a short deal.
5. **Pace** for attackers and wingers (counter-attacking needs it).
6. **Contract:** short for older players, none beyond the season unless the player is a young starter.

## 6. Checks before every deal

- Wage fits (section 4, rule 1) and the 90% cap still holds after the sale.
- The player is not already signed elsewhere or retired.
- A starter or high-POT player is sold only at about 1.5x value or more, with cover in place.
- Fees you pay (sign-on, agent) still leave part of the transfer budget in reserve.
