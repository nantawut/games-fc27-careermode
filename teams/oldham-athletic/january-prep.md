## 4. How I will rank them

1. **Wage first, at the real wage scale from section 0.** Reject anyone whose wage is more than the wage freed by the paired sale plus the room in section 2. No stretching past the €15K budget.
2. **Upgrade size.** Must beat the current starter in that slot by at least 2 OVR. A cover signing needs to be within 3 OVR of the starter and as cheap as possible. At Futship wages the cheapest cover is about €1.6K to €2.5K, so one cover signing may be the most the January room allows.
3. **Gap priority.** Fix a slot with **one real option** before upgrading a slot with two. After that, promotion order from `plan.md`: striker, then holding midfielder, then wingers, then goalkeeper, then centre-back.
4. **Age and resale.** Tie-break toward younger, higher-POT players (the Zegarra, Moungam and Al Oui profile). Over 32 is accepted only for a promotion-critical slot and a short deal.
5. **Pace** for attackers and wingers (counter-attacking needs it).
6. **Contract:** short for older players, none beyond the season unless the player is a young starter.

## 6. Checks before every deal

- Wage fits (section 4, rule 1) and the bill stays within the €15K budget after the sale.
- The squad stays at 22 players or more, or the reason it cannot is written down. The Zegarra order already left it at 20.
- The player is not already signed elsewhere or retired.
- A starter or high-POT player is sold only at about 1.5x value or more, with cover in place.
- Fees you pay (sign-on, agent) still leave part of the transfer budget in reserve.
