Default formation: 4-2-3-1 (fit check)

4-2-3-1 is the default shape (your choice, 2026-11-25). The earlier default, 4-1-4-1, is now the fallback. This is my own fit check against the squad data, not sourced, and the formation has not been tested.

  • Double pivot: Norburn (CM, CDM, 63/67) plus Pett (62/65) or Chilvers (62/66) or Appiah-Forson (61/65, loan flag), who all list CDM as a secondary position. It needs a second holding midfielder, so the holding-midfielder signing (priority 2) matters more. A double pivot needs two, not one.
  • Attacking midfielder: Chilvers (CM, CAM, CDM, 62/66), Payne (CM, CAM, 59/67) or Hammond (CM, CAM, 57/65). The squad has no natural CAM above 59 apart from Kavanagh, so this slot is the weakest.
  • Wide players: Stevens (LM) and Drummond (RM) in the three behind the striker. Striker: Kavanagh, with Fondop and Nevitt as cover.
  • Fallback to 4-1-4-1: use it if the second holding midfielder does not arrive, or if the weak attacking-midfield slot costs results. Test in early league games, since EA's own notes say no formation dominates.
  • Recruitment effect: the 4-2-3-1 makes a second holding midfielder and a better attacking midfielder more important. Recruitment priorities have not been changed.
  • Community setup (FUT.GG, Ultimate Team data, unverified for Career Mode): Balanced build-up, Balanced defensive line, line height 60 (pros 65), and a shared tactic code sQEWL2e&2qYt. Counter build-up is used by only 18%, so a counter-attack style on this shape is something to test. Part of the page was read through a summary, so check it directly at FUT.GG.

Variant to test: 4-2-3-1 Wide

  • Difference: the wide slots are LM and RM (Inside Forward, Balanced is the most-used role: 58% LM, 56% RM) instead of the standard shape's LAM and RAM (Shadow Striker). Only about 3% of community squads use it (the page's #7 formation).
  • Why it might fit: Stevens (LM, LW) and Drummond (RM, ST, LM, RW) list LM and RM as natural positions. This is my reasoning; how the game scores out-of-position players is unchecked.
  • Catch: Inside Forward pulls wide players inward, but counter-attacking needs width and pace. Use Winger (Balanced), a less common option at 13% to 14% on the page.
  • Unknown: the page does not say who the formation suits, so it is not shown to be better than the standard shape.
  • Decision rule: keep it only if results or the wide players' in-game ratings in those slots look better than the standard version.

Staff

Hire coaches and scouts first, then the Director of Football once the budget allows. Coaches now assess Potential only, which suits a youth-and-resale model. Other roles and costs are unverified.

Money and squad rules

  • Selling starters and high-POT players: only for about 1.5× value or more, with a replacement already identified. Never sell a starter in the last days of a window without cover.
  • Selling surplus non-starters: exempt from the 1.5× test. Sell at fair value, but only once a replacement or cover exists.
  • Wages: keep the bill at or below 90% of the wage budget (€13.5K/week).
  • Contracts: new deals only for starters and high-resale players.
  • Squad: 22 to 24 players, two options per position; rotate when fatigue or morale drops.
  • Youth growth: give prospects minutes in cup games and easier fixtures.
  • Loans (Fawunmi POT 74, Tahir POT 71): use them in year one. They end in June, so build nothing on keeping them; try a permanent deal only if the price is clearly low.

Development: there is no per-player training in FC 27

The official Pitch Notes say the Training Plan is removed except Energy Recovery, and coaches no longer improve attributes directly (they assess Potential only). So there is no training to set for each player. Instead:

  • Match minutes: the main driver. Give prospects games in cup matches and easier fixtures.
  • Match fitness and morale: rotate before fatigue sets in, because both feed Dynamic OVR (form, morale and match fitness).
  • Energy Recovery: use it for tired starters before big matches.
  • Growth Profile: check each signing's Growth Profile (six types) in game before buying.
  • Coaches: hire for accuracy of Potential assessment, not for training.
