Team tactic summary (my opinion, untested)

Built from the community sources and the squad data. No source has Oldham-level evidence, and shape matters less than player quality: a 62-rated side on Legendary cannot be rescued by a formation.

  • Shape: 4-2-3-1 Wide as the default, the standard 4-2-3-1 as the comparison, 4-1-4-1 as the fallback.
  • Build-up and line: Balanced build-up (the community's Counter build-up is only about 15% to 18%, so test it, but start Balanced). Defensive line starts at 50, not the community's 60, because the back four is short on pace and a high line is the bigger risk.
  • Roles: Winger (Balanced) on both wings, Advanced Forward at striker, one Deep-Lying Playmaker and one Holding in the double pivot, Playmaker at CAM, Defend on the centre-backs and fullbacks.
  • Game plan: sit compact, win the ball, then play quickly to the wingers and the lone striker. The wide players and striker are all about 62, so pace is the thing to check first. If they cannot get away from defenders, counter-attacking will not work and Balanced possession may be safer.
  • Biggest risk: the board wants promotion, and a weak squad plus a high-variance style is a risky mix. Judge the tactic by points after the first ten league games.

Money and squad rules

  • Selling starters and high-POT players: only for about 1.5× value or more, with a replacement already identified. Never sell a starter in the last days of a window without cover.
  • Selling surplus non-starters: exempt from the 1.5× test. Sell at fair value, but only once a replacement or cover exists.
  • Wages: keep the bill at or below 90% of the wage budget (€13.5K/week).
  • Contracts: new deals only for starters and high-resale players.
  • Squad: 22 to 24 players, two options per position; rotate when fatigue or morale drops.
  • Youth growth: give prospects minutes in cup games and easier fixtures.
  • Loans (Fawunmi POT 74, Tahir POT 71): use them in year one. They end in June, so build nothing on keeping them; try a permanent deal only if the price is clearly low.

January window

Window: 1 to 31 January. January's free agents cannot be named in advance: AI clubs sign players after the summer and unsigned free agents can retire. The Futship lists below cover only the first summer.

  1. Before the window: count the squad against the two-options-per-position rule and list every slot down to one real option.
  2. Fill first-window gaps first: striker, holding midfielder, wide midfielders, third goalkeeper, centre-back depth. Promotion is the objective, so a striker or holding midfielder outranks depth.
  3. 1 January: get the in-game free-agent list and send it for ranking against the squad, the wage cap and the gaps.
  4. Money: spend only what is left of the transfer budget and keep a reserve (the 70% cap applied to the first window only). Wages are the main limit: assume each signing needs a sale first. The 90% wage cap is about €13.5K/week.
  5. Sales: starters and high-POT players only at about 1.5x value or more with a replacement identified. Surplus non-starters are exempt.
  6. Loans end in June, so none remain in January.
  7. Likely leftovers (unverified guess): older, high-wage players. Check each in game; they may have signed elsewhere or retired.
  8. Deadline: sign by about 25 January so a failed deal can be fixed before the 31 January close.

Development: there is no per-player training in FC 27

The official Pitch Notes say the Training Plan is removed except Energy Recovery, and coaches no longer improve attributes directly (they assess Potential only). So there is no training to set for each player. Instead:

  • Match minutes: the main driver. Give prospects games in cup matches and easier fixtures.
  • Match fitness and morale: rotate before fatigue sets in, because both feed Dynamic OVR (form, morale and match fitness).
  • Energy Recovery: use it for tired starters before big matches.
  • Growth Profile: check each signing's Growth Profile (six types) in game before buying.
  • Coaches: hire for accuracy of Potential assessment, not for training.
