A short January window (1 January to 31 January) gives a second chance to fix problems, sell surplus players and replace injured ones.

Money and squad rules

  • Selling starters and high-POT players: only for about 1.5× value or more, with a replacement already identified. Never sell a starter in the last days of a window without cover.
  • Selling surplus non-starters: exempt from the 1.5× test. Sell at fair value, but only once a replacement or cover exists.
  • Wages: keep the bill at or below 90% of the wage budget (€13.5K/week).
  • Contracts: new deals only for starters and high-resale players.
  • Squad: 22 to 24 players, two options per position; rotate when fatigue or morale drops.
  • Youth growth: give prospects minutes in cup games and easier fixtures.
  • Loans (Fawunmi POT 74, Tahir POT 71): use them in year one. They end in June, so build nothing on keeping them; try a permanent deal only if the price is clearly low.

Development: there is no per-player training in FC 27

The official Pitch Notes say the Training Plan is removed except Energy Recovery, and coaches no longer improve attributes directly (they assess Potential only). So there is no training to set for each player. Instead:

  • Match minutes: the main driver. Give prospects games in cup matches and easier fixtures.
  • Match fitness and morale: rotate before fatigue sets in, because both feed Dynamic OVR (form, morale and match fitness).
  • Energy Recovery: use it for tired starters before big matches.
  • Growth Profile: check each signing's Growth Profile (six types) in game before buying.
  • Coaches: hire for accuracy of Potential assessment, not for training.
