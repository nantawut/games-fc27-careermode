Executive summary

  • Target: promotion in season 1. This matches the board's objective (raised from a play-off push on 2026-11-24).
  • Money is tight: €1.2M transfer budget and a €15K/week wage budget, both confirmed in game. The squad's wage bill is already about €13.7K/week (derived from community data), so every signing must be wage-neutral or paired with a departure.

Money and squad rules

  • Selling: only for about 1.5× value or more, with a replacement already identified. Never sell a starter in the last days of a window without cover.
  • Wages: keep the bill at or below 90% of the wage budget (€13.5K/week).
  • Contracts: new deals only for starters and high-resale players.
  • Squad: 22 to 24 players, two options per position; rotate when fatigue or morale drops.
  • Youth growth: give prospects minutes in cup games and easier fixtures.
  • Loans (Fawunmi POT 74, Tahir POT 71): use them in year one. They end in June, so build nothing on keeping them; try a permanent deal only if the price is clearly low.
